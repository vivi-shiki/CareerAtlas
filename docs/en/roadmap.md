## Scope and references

Registration, login/logout, three-language navigation, version-conflict recovery, and unsaved-change protection support these workflows. See [quick start](./quickstart.md), [privacy](./privacy-and-ai.md), and [troubleshooting](./troubleshooting.md). Current limits include text-only practice, Markdown-only export, and latest-100 P1 lists.
